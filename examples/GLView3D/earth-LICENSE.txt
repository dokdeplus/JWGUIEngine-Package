The world map on the globe: Natural Earth II (shaded relief, land cover,
water), made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @
naturalearthdata.com. Natural Earth data is in the public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The 2048 x 1024 picture was put together from the Natural Earth II tiles
that ship with CesiumJS (packages/engine/Source/Assets/Textures/NaturalEarthII,
level 2), which CesiumJS lists as public domain data from Natural Earth.

The globe, its stand and everything else in the example are drawn by the
example's own code.
